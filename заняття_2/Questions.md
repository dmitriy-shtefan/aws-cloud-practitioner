## What is the primary distinction between fixed costs and variable costs in cloud economics?

- [ ] Fixed costs are incurred based on actual resource usage, while variable costs remain constant.
- [ ] Fixed costs are predictable and stable, while variable costs fluctuate based on resource consumption.
- [ ] Fixed costs include on-demand services, while variable costs encompass long-term commitments.
- [ ] Fixed costs are associated with cloud environments, while variable costs are tied to on-premises infrastructure.
